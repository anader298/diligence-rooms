Before the microphone turns on

The companion records and transcribes what it hears. In many places, including the US two-party consent states (such as California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania and Washington), everyone in the conversation must agree first.

Without consent, use Ask: all four cards and Think still work from typed questions.
