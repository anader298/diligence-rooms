Run sheet, 45 minutes

  1. 0 to 5: introductions. Ninety seconds on the line from the one card.
  2. 5 to 30: the buying group's questions. Number, risk, first step. Stop.
  3. 30 to 40: a scenario, probably the overlap cities.
  4. 40 to 45: three questions from the one card, in order.

Twelve questions in the buyers' voices

Dan Ellison

  1. "Nine terminals, three overlap cities. Where do you start?"
  2. "Tell me about a model you built that was wrong."
  3. "Who wins when the model and the operator disagree?"
  4. "What would you not touch in ninety days?"

Theo Lindqvist

  1. "What would you need from my two analysts in month one?"
  2. "How do you keep a network model current after the study is done?"
  3. "What did you learn from the call with me two weeks ago?"
  4. "How would you work with Sam's team on day one?"

Ines Carvalho

  1. "Define cost per shipment. What is in it, what is out?"
  2. "Tailspin allocates terminal overhead differently. How do you compare?"
  3. "What saving would you be willing to sign your name to?"
  4. "How do you treat a lease with two years left?"

Four push-back drills

  • "That network was a quarter of our size." Answer with what scales and what does not.
  • "We already had a consultant do this." Ask what was implemented, then say what is different about owning it.
  • "You haven't worked for a fund-owned company." Say it is true, then say what we would ask the CFO in week one.
  • "Why not just close all three Tailspin buildings?" Service and lease terms. Neither is public. Say so.

One mini-case

Overlap city A: Northwind owns a 180,000 sq ft warehouse at 70% utilisation; Tailspin leases a 60,000 sq ft cross-dock at 90%. Both serve the same three customers. Recommend in two minutes.

Model answer: keep the cross-dock's flow, move it into the owned building if the dock count allows, exit the lease at its break date. First step: the lease date and the dock count, neither of which is public.

Grading rule

Answers are graded by a reviewer who did not write the question. Self-grading agreed with itself in an earlier room; it is no longer allowed. Score each answer on three things: a number, a risk, a first step.
