3. Worldview

Networks drift toward too many buildings because no one owns the decision to close one. Density beats reach. Service is the constraint, cost is the objective. Inference from the three quotes above and the 2025 letter.

4. What he will probe

  • A time you recommended closing or consolidating something, and what happened to service.
  • How you would build cost per shipment across two companies that count it differently.
  • What you do when the operations leader disagrees with the model.
